
Good question! Let me think about what analytics we can realistically offer linked businesses:
What We Can Track (Platform-Side)
For linked businesses, we have visibility into:
1. Profile Views - How many people viewed their Ilthres business profile
2. Outbound Clicks - How many people clicked through to their external website
3. Search Appearances - How often they appear in platform search results
4. Geographic Data - Where the viewers are located
5. Referral Sources - How people found their profile (direct search, category browsing, etc.)
What We Can't Track (External Site)
We don't have access to:
- Time spent on their external website
- Bounce rates
- Conversion rates
- Page views on their actual site
- User behavior after they leave Ilthres
The Value Proposition
For linked businesses, analytics would be limited compared to hosted businesses — we can only track what happens on the Ilthres platform, not what happens on their external site.
This could be:
Analytics Tiers:
1. Basic — Profile views + outbound clicks only
2. Enhanced — Add search appearances, geographic data, referral sources (for both linked and hosted businesses)
