About

Sakura Japanese Restaurant brings authentic Japanese cuisine to the heart of Manhattan. Our chefs trained in Tokyo bring traditional techniques with a modern twist. We source the freshest fish daily from Tsukiji-trained suppliers and serve the finest sushi, sashimi, and specialty rolls in New York City.
